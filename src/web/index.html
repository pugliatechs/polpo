About your Builder Profile

A statistical mirror of how you work with AI coding agents, computed entirely on this machine from the session transcripts polpo already has on disk.

Privacy

Nothing leaves the machine. No LLM call, no telemetry, no third party. The profile is just counts and ratios derived from local files. The only text in the response is your archetype name and a one-line blurb, both hardcoded in the analyzer source.

The five dimensions (each 0–100)

  • Steering — how closely you guide the agent turn by turn (prompts per session, prompt length, command-style verbs).
  • Execution — hands-on output volume per session (edits + shell runs).
  • Engineering — rigor signals (tests run, commits made, read/search share of the tool mix).
  • Product Instinct — range and user-focused language (unique projects, product-shaped vocabulary).
  • Planning — plan-tool use, structured prompts, code-block ratio.

How to read it

Treat the scores as a mirror, not a verdict. Scores saturate (value/(value+midpoint)*100), so 50 means "comfortable amount of this", 80 means "a lot of this", and 100 is asymptotic. Use it for self-reflection or drift detection ("my test-run count fell to zero last month"), not for comparisons.

Heuristic transparency

Every signal that feeds a dimension is documented inline in src/server/profile-analyzer.js. If the scores feel off, the math is around 200 lines you can read and tune.

Limitations

  • Statistical, not semantic — the analyzer counts, it doesn't understand.
  • Custom MCP tools without the mcp__ prefix land in the other bucket.
  • Sessions beyond a 200-session content cap or a 15s deadline are still counted in metadata aggregates but don't feed tool/shell/prompt stats. analyzedSessions vs totalSessions shows the truncation.
